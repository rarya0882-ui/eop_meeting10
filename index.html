STATION 2 • SINTA'S TRUTH CHECK TUTORIAL

The 3 Rules: Never Pitch Fake Data to Committee

[F] FACT (Fakta Terbukti)

• Real historical data.
• Already happened in the past.
• Proven by official documents.
e.g. "Brief confirmed Room 204."

[P] PROPOSAL (Usulan / Solusi)

• An action idea for the FUTURE.
• Not implemented yet.
• Uses words: should, propose, MAY.
e.g. "Checklist MAY reduce errors."

[U] UNSUPPORTED (Klaim Bohong)

• Fake numbers or false victory.
• Claiming 100% success BEFORE trial.
• AI hallucinated metrics.
e.g. "Attendance jumped 30%!"
